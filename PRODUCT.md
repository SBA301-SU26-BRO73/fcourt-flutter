# Product

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

FCOURT helps Vietnamese court owners manage branch employees and helps staff
operate bookings, check-in, checkout and counter sales on Android phones/tablets.
Staff need clear, quick actions while moving between courts, including poor light
and intermittent connectivity.

## Design direction

Preserve the existing green Material 3 identity and system typography. Use
Vietnamese labels, light/dark themes and familiar accessible controls. The owner
approved extending the current design where it improves these workflows.

## Principles

- Show authoritative results and distinguish pending work from success.
- Keep the next operational action near the booking or product.
- Preserve input and command identity across recoverable network failures.
- Use text/icons alongside colors, scalable text and 48dp touch targets.
- Keep offline decisions clearly separate from server-confirmed admission.
